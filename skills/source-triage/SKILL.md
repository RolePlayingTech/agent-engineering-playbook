# Source triage

## Procedure
1. Record original URL, author, publication date, access date and source type.
2. Extract specific claims and distinguish observed measurements from opinion or vendor promotion.
3. Identify environment, model, sample size and limitations; mark missing details unknown.
4. Compare with an independent source when practical.
5. Propose a reproducible experiment and failure condition.
6. Classify as **tested locally**, **primary-source recommendation**, **third-party observation**, or **hypothesis**.
7. Publish a short synthesis in research/, linking sources and avoiding lengthy quotations.

## Output
Claim, evidence classification, applicability, limitations, proposed test and revisit trigger.

## Limits
Do not label a recommendation locally tested without recorded results. Do not turn a single anecdote into a universal rule.
